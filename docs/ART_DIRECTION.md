# LEVERAGE — Art Direction

**Identity in one line:** *rain-slick industrial noir, forged not bought.*
Everything the player sees — every brick, window grid, face, decal, spark —
is original procedural content generated by `tools/forge/` Python scripts.
No asset-store kits, no generic low-poly look: the game's distinctive
identity comes from a hand-tuned palette, material language and lighting
model applied consistently across a fully generated asset set.

---

## 1. Palette

- **Base night:** near-blue blacks `#0a0c12`, wet asphalt `#191a1e`,
  concrete `#3a3a38`, soot brick `#4a2f2a`.
- **Signature accent:** ember amber `#F2B840` (UI gold, sodium lamps,
  mission markers) against **alert crimson** `#E84038` (wanted stars,
  taillights, enemy markers) and **neon teal** `#3BE8B0` (Neon Mile,
  POI door markers, radio static).
- **District moods:** Kiln Row = warm brick + sodium amber; Ashfall Docks =
  cold steel blue + rust; The Furnace = soot black + refinery orange; Neon
  Mile = magenta/teal signage on black; Slagworks = ash grey + hazard
  yellow; Crown Heights = cold glass white + deep shadow.
- Skin tones: 8-tone ramp from deep umber to pale rose, deliberately
  saturated-warm so faces read at mobile distance.

## 2. Texture language (96 world textures)

All forged power-of-two and tileable, **256–2048²** — measured census: 68×512²,
18×256², 5×128², 2×1024² (`windows_atlas`, `road_marks`), 2×2048²
(`signs_atlas`, `posters_atlas`), 1×64². The governing mobile rules are the
4096² cap and the non-POT repeat risk, so every texture here is POT and none
exceeds 2048²; an earlier revision of this line claimed "512–1024²", which was
wrong at both ends. All 96 now import VRAM-compressed with mipmaps (D-25) —
see `docs/ASSET_REGISTER.md`.

- **Surfaces:** asphalt (aggregate noise + cracks), concrete (stained,
  panel lines), brick red/dark/grey (mortar course variation + per-brick
  value jitter), stucco, corrugated metal, rusted metal, tile, wood
  planks, carpet, grass ×3, water.
- **Facades:** 8 building skins (brick ×3, concrete, stucco, glass, metal,
  corrugated) generated as window-grid systems: lit/unlit window cells
  with interior warmth variance, plus **matching `_e` emission maps** so
  night windows glow without extra lights — the city's night identity is
  one material flag flip (`WorldGen.set_night_emission`).
- **Decals:** blood, scorch, glass, oil, manhole, crosswalk paint, lane
  marks — pooled quads projected on impact.
- **Particles:** spark, blood, smoke, debris, muzzle — unshaded billboard
  quads with vertex-color-over-albedo so pooled systems can recolor per
  burst kind.
- **Faces:** 24 procedural portrait albedos (feature placement varies by
  seed: brow, eye spacing, nose, jaw shading, stubble/scars/lip tone) so
  every named character and random ped reads as a *person*, not a mannequin.

## 3. Character look

Blocky-soft: capsule/box/cylinder primitives skinned to a 22-bone rig,
deliberately *not* realistic — chunky jackets, tapered trousers, heavy
boots; silhouette-first design so combat reads at 3.4 m camera distance.
17 hair styles (crop, buzz, fade, slick, braids, locs, cap, hood, long,
pony, bun, afro, bandana, beanie, mohawk, bald, tied) × 8 hair colors ×
8 skins × 24 faces × 6 outfit color sets = effectively unique citizens.
Faction silhouettes: Family = flat caps and dock jackets (warm browns);
Corp = armor-vested security (cold grey + white trim); Crew = syndicate
reds and blacks; police = near-black cruisers + red/blue bar.

## 4. Lighting model (Mobile renderer discipline)

- One shadowed DirectionalLight3D: angle/color/energy driven by the 24 h
  clock; dusk lerp amber→indigo; weather dims (rain ×0.45, overcast ×0.7).
- ProceduralSky gradient re-tinted per frame (night/dusk/day + weather
  blend) — the sky is part of the mood system, not a backdrop asset.
- Night = emissive facades + pooled 28 OmniLights snapped to the nearest
  lamp posts (sodium amber in old districts, teal-white in Neon Mile,
  cold white in Crown Heights) + glow. Fog thickens at night and in rain.
- Interiors: one warm omni + emissive strip materials; deliberately dim
  and cozy against the wet blue outside.
- Muzzle flashes are 70 ms pooled omnis; police bars alternate red/blue
  emissive at 0.4 s; traffic lights are night-visible emissive omnis.

## 5. Camera & framing

Third-person orbit, 3.4 m spring, collision-aware (pulls in against
geometry, mask 1|4), pitch clamp −55°..+35°, FOV 68 with combat kicks
(sprint/dodge/hits nudge +2..6°), lock-on reframes the target, cinematic
override (Cutscene takes the camera with letterbox bars). The camera is
tuned for one-hand play: look is drag-anywhere-right, never a second stick.

## 6. UI art

Diegetic-noir flat design: 9-slice panels with 2 px ember-gold hairlines,
DejaVu Sans (Bitstream Vera licensed) at high weights, gold-on-near-black
typography, red reserved exclusively for danger (wanted stars, enemy
markers, HP), green for money/confirm, teal for information. Touch buttons
are ≥44 px circles with gold rims that brighten on press. Minimap and
phone map are *drawn* (roads, POI dots, mission ring, player arrow) — no
render-to-texture, no fake dashboards: every element shows real game state.

## 7. Audio identity (companion to the look)

Radio-noir: 15 original tracks (down-tempo noir keys, dockside percussion,
neon synth, furnace drone, finale swell); 138 SFX forged by additive/
noise synthesis (wet impacts, distant thunder, sodium-buzz ambience);
voice is stylized — 60 bark blips per gender/color, subtitles always on by
default. The city sounds like it looks: rain on everything, warmth
underneath.

## 8. Anti-asset-flip guarantees

- Zero downloaded 3D models/textures/audio; the only third-party files in
  the repo are DejaVu fonts (license text in `docs/LICENSES.md`).
- Deterministic forge scripts (`tools/forge/`) mean every asset is
  reproducible from source with recorded parameters — the asset register
  lists generator + seed family for each file.
- Consistent material grammar (window-grid facades, ember accents, wet
  surfaces) across all six districts ties generated content into one
  authored look.


## Concept render (visual target)

`docs/gameplay_concept.png` (1170×2532, iPhone portrait) is the hand-built
visual target for a Kiln Row night frame: rain, wet-asphalt reflections,
sodium-amber lamp cones vs teal Neon Mile bleed, emissive window grids,
third-person Eli with amber/teal rim light, and the full touch HUD laid out
per `docs/CONTROLS.md` §2. It is generated reproducibly by
`tools/concept/render_concept.py` (pure numpy/Pillow software renderer —
pinhole camera, z-buffered quads, additive glow, screen-space wet reflection).

HONESTY NOTE: this is an art-direction TARGET, not engine output. The
delivery sandbox is headless with no GPU/rasterizer (KNOWN_DEFECTS K-08);
the on-device Godot mobile renderer produces the real frame, which should be
compared against this target during the VERIFICATION_GUIDE §B pass.
